Projects

hieroglitch (2020 — present)

Eurorack modular synthesis project spanning industrial techno, ambient breaks, and textural sound design. Recordings are predominantly live sessions recorded direct from the modular system, later mastered and released. Earlier releases were produced in Ableton. All artwork and visual identity self-produced.

  • 13 releases to date on Bandcamp and all major streaming platforms
  • Press coverage in Discipline Magazine (December 2025)
  • Remix credit: Lost [Hieroglitch Remix] for Memorrhage
