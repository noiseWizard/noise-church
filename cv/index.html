hieroglitch (2020 — present)

Eurorack modular synthesis project spanning industrial techno, rhythmic noise, ambient breaks, and textural sound design.

Improvised sessions recorded direct from eurorack modular systems. If necessary, edits to recordings occur.

Earlier releases produced in Ableton.

All artwork and visual identity self-produced.

  • 13 releases on Bandcamp and all major streaming platforms
  • Press coverage in Discipline Magazine (December 2025)
  • Remix credit: Lost [Hieroglitch Remix] for Memorrhage

Worgor (2017 — 2019, on hiatus)

Dark ambient, industrial doom, and harsh noise project produced in Ableton with live guitar.

Live performances built around guitar, effects pedals, and an Elektron Octatrack.

  • 4 releases including a self-released cassette
  • Opened for Trepaneringsritualen, Melbourne — July 16, 2019

Commissioned Work (pre-2017, resuming)

Sound design and composition for tabletop roleplaying campaigns.

Created bespoke ambient and atmospheric music to order, working with dungeon masters to achieve specific emotional responses in players rather than simply providing background noise.

Work ranged from environmental soundscapes to combat music incorporating elements such as glitched breakbeats and infrasound to create tension and disorientation.

Consultation included guidance on the importance of appropriate playback equipment and how sound system quality directly impacts the intended atmosphere and player experience.

Weekly Beats (2018 — present)

A year-long challenge to write and release one new track per week. Each year is treated as an opportunity to experiment — new modules, different routing methods, and unfamiliar approaches are deliberately introduced. Genres and techniques evolve continuously throughout the year rather than remaining fixed.

  • 2018 — 26 weeks
  • 2020 — 5 weeks
  • 2022 — 26 weeks
  • 2024 — 52 weeks
  • 2026 — in progress
